What's this all about?

Unnamed is a nonpartisan website aimed to help volunteers trying to cure ballots and voters who need their ballots cured. This website aims to provide information on how to cure a ballot in each county. To cure a ballot means to resolve issues with a rejected ballot. Curing ballots can sometimes help tip a close race.
